Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify the authenticity of my Silk Mark Saree?

Every pure silk saree from Aruvi Collections includes an authentic Silk Mark India QR certificate tag with a unique serial number that can be verified directly on the Silk Mark India portal.

What is the return and exchange policy?

We offer a 7-day hassle-free return and exchange policy for unused items in original luxury packaging with seals intact. Reverse pickup is fully complimentary across India.

Can I request custom blouse stitching?

Yes! You can contact our boutique master tailor via WhatsApp right after placing your order to customize measurements, necklines, and embroidery patterns.
